# Jarvis Intelligence — decision and delegation system
Nate's request: continuously research business improvements and opportunities across industries, identify weaknesses in his operations, and do useful preparatory work without repeated prompts. Present only specific, material actions with evidence. Build and deploy immediately using existing authorized systems.

## Product contract
Add /intelligence without replacing the dashboard or Workshop. Top of page: at most three ranked current decisions. Secondary tabs/filters: Act now, Ready for review, Watch, Outcomes. Every item has an imperative action, why it matters now, exact next step, owner, current evidence/source links and timestamps, confidence, any real deadline, effort/capital when known, and a transparent impact basis. Dollar balances, revenue, scenario estimates, and actual outcomes must never be conflated. Unknown values stay unknown. A priority score is a transparent heuristic, never a probability of profit.

Two independent loops: server operational scan every 15 minutes; hosted research analyst hourly across customer demand, social/search signals, regulatory/procurement changes, AI/automation and cross-industry business models. The analyst reads current settings and recent findings, verifies primary sources, records completed runs even with zero qualified findings, and writes only new/material changes. At most three external findings per run. No generic news dump. Show stale/failed/partial coverage honestly and distinguish a scheduled task from an observed execution.

Internal diagnostic coverage: overdue operational receivables; sent quotes lacking recorded response; planned job revenue per budgeted hour against editable target initially $130/hour; unresolved past job statuses and missing actual time/cost preventing reliable profitability; near-term schedule missing start times and crew capacity uncertainty. Exclude deleted/cancelled/void/draft where appropriate and confirmed demo records. Do not infer empty sources, completed work, available crew or verified accounting profit. Read current Homeworks sync freshness separately. Keep QuickBooks coverage explicitly unverified unless actually read.

External research covers current WeedEater advantages and credible opportunities beyond landscaping. Qualifying action must connect evidence to a monetization/cost-saving mechanism, fit, entry barrier, cheapest validation step, success metric and stop rule. Search interest is not paid demand. Legislative proposals are not enacted rules. Tender listings are not awarded contracts. Never fabricate deadlines, capital, ROI or private company practices. Use public documented operating principles only as design references.

## Delegation
Automatically prepare tangible review packets for qualifying internal findings: an unsent collections/follow-up draft where real record facts support it; an exact calculation table for pricing; a short data-repair worksheet for an operational gap. External analyst produces a researched experiment brief, source comparison or unsent outreach template where appropriate. Persist actual prepared content and timestamp, identify who must act next, and distinguish prepared from sent/executed. Owner can accept/watch/dismiss/complete, add outcome notes and separately record realized revenue/savings/time. Decisions persist and repeated scans must not reset them. Revenue attribution remains owner-reported, not proven causal lift. There is no profit guarantee or claim to cover every possible source.

## Technical boundaries
Existing Next 16.3.8/Supabase owner-auth pattern; no new paid provider, secrets in client, new public write endpoint, or business-source record mutations. Preserve Homeworks authority and existing voice/Workshop. New tables enforce RLS, active-owner isolation, bounded inputs, and service-only ingestion/worker RPCs. Worker endpoints use the existing CRON_SECRET. Native app mutations remain authenticated and same-origin. Research uses existing ChatGPT tools and verified Supabase connector; no dependency on PC uptime. Do not weaken access controls; this prevents corrupting decisions, irrespective of user indifference to security.

Bound all scans, imports and run histories; reject oversized partial reads, stale/unsupported evidence, unsafe URLs and invalid states. Use stable dedup keys. Overlapping runs must not duplicate cards or regress latest facts; atomic leases and source-run identity needed. Record connector limitations and read failures; retain last good result but mark it stale. No subscriptions, purchases, customer messages, ads or investment transactions.

## Verification
Actual migrations tested under PostgreSQL/PGlite including owner isolation and service-only functions. Pure diagnostic/ranking/validation tests for deleted/demo inputs, no hours, zero prices, stale failures, dedup/status persistence, unknown impacts, and expired evidence. UI desktop/390px mobile tests for ranking, detail expansion, source links, preferences, decision persistence, prepared packets and output tracking. Production must show real internal findings, a completed external research run with sources, the enabled recurrence and successful worker receipt; physical device claims excluded.


## Public operating-model references
- Amazon’s 2016 shareholder letter describes making reversible decisions quickly and measuring customer results rather than treating process completion as success: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2016-letter-to-shareholders . Jarvis applies this through inexpensive validation tests and observed outcomes; this is an adaptation, not access to Amazon’s internal systems.
- GitLab’s public DRI handbook assigns clear ownership and decision responsibility: https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/directly-responsible-individuals/ . Jarvis shows the next actor and prepared work explicitly instead of claiming a collection of autonomous executives.
- Existing daily five-story news task will be upgraded to an hourly action-only research watch, avoiding a second overlapping news feed. Existing personal morning briefing remains intact.
